# 🚗 Fix My Ride

> **Smart Vehicle Service & Management System**

Fix My Ride is a full-stack vehicle service management platform designed to make vehicle servicing **simple, fast, and convenient**. Customers can book vehicle services, manage their vehicles, track service status, and view their service history, while mechanics and administrators can efficiently manage the complete service workflow.
